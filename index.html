02 / A MOMENT OF CLARITY

A small shift.
A stable arrangement.

Magnesium doesn’t need to gain six electrons. Losing its two outer electrons reveals a shell that’s already full.

Mg2 | 8 | 2NEUTRAL ATOM
− 2 e⁻
Mg²⁺2 | 8STABLE ION
The second shell becomes the outermost occupied shell. Eight is the destination, not the starting address.

In this chapter’s simplified shell model. Lose electrons → more positive; gain electrons → more negative.
