The magnesium idea in one picture

The outer shell does not always already contain eight electrons. Eight is usually the stable destination in the simplified model from this chapter.

Mg neutral: 2 | 8 | 2
→ lose 2 e⁻ →
Mg²⁺: 2 | 8
The trick: once magnesium loses the two electrons in shell 3, that empty shell is no longer the outermost occupied shell. Shell 2 becomes the valence shell, and it already has eight.

Rule of thumb for this chapter: lose electrons → more positive; gain electrons → more negative.
